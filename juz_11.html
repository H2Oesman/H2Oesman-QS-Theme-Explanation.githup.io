Gambaran Umum Juz 11

Juz 11 memuat Surah At-Taubah ayat 94–129, Surah Yunus secara lengkap, dan Surah Hud ayat 1–5. Penutup At-Taubah membahas alasan-alasan orang yang tidak ikut Perang Tabuk, Masjid Dhirar, tobat tiga sahabat yang tertinggal, perintah menuntut ilmu agama, dan sifat kasih sayang Rasulullah. Surah Yunus menegaskan kebenaran wahyu, tanda-tanda kekuasaan Allah di alam, kebebasan beriman, dan kisah kaum Nabi Yunus yang selamat karena bertobat. Juz ini ditutup dengan awal Surah Hud yang menyeru beristigfar dan bertobat.

Yaa ayyuhan-naasu qad jaa'atkum mau'izhatum mir rabbikum wa syifaa'ul limaa fish-shuduuri wa hudaw wa rahmatul lil-mu'miniin
Terjemahan: "Wahai manusia! Sungguh, telah datang kepadamu pelajaran (Al-Qur'an) dari Tuhanmu, penyembuh bagi penyakit yang ada dalam dada dan petunjuk serta rahmat bagi orang yang beriman." (QS Yunus [10]: 57)
Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Setelah diingatkan bahwa semua yang hidup akan kembali kepada Allah, manusia diberi tahu: telah datang kepadamu pelajaran dari Tuhanmu, yaitu Al-Qur'an, penyembuh bagi penyakit dalam dada, petunjuk, serta rahmat bagi orang-orang yang beriman. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 57)

1. Surah At-Taubah (Pengampunan) · Ayat 94–129

Penutup Surah At-Taubah menguraikan sikap orang-orang Badui dan munafik yang mencari-cari alasan untuk tidak ikut Perang Tabuk, sekaligus memuji kaum Muhajirin dan Ansar generasi pertama. Allah melarang salat di Masjid Dhirar yang dibangun untuk memecah belah, menerima tobat tiga sahabat yang tertinggal setelah mereka merasakan bumi yang luas terasa sempit, dan memerintahkan sebagian orang beriman untuk mendalami agama. Surah ditutup dengan sifat Rasulullah yang sangat menginginkan keselamatan umatnya dan penuh kasih sayang.

94–99Orang Munafik dan Kemunafikan MerekaAllah mengancam orang munafik dengan azab di dunia dan akhirat. Mereka suka bersumpah palsu, takut diketahui aibnya, dan lebih memilih dunia daripada akhirat.

Pesan Akidah, Ibadah, dan Akhlak

Akidah: Allah menerima tobat hamba-Nya dan menerima sedekah (ayat 104); Allah membeli jiwa dan harta orang beriman dengan surga (ayat 111).
Ibadah: Bertobat dengan sungguh-sungguh (ayat 117–118), bersedekah untuk menyucikan diri (ayat 103), dan mendalami ilmu agama (ayat 122).
Akhlak: Jujur dan bersama orang-orang yang benar (ayat 119), tidak memecah belah umat (ayat 107), dan meneladani kasih sayang Rasul (ayat 128).

2. Surah Yunus (Yunus) · Ayat 1–109

Yunus adalah surah ke-10, terdiri atas 109 ayat, dan tergolong surah Makkiyah. Surah ini diturunkan menjelang akhir periode Makkah dan dibuka dengan huruf alif laam raa. Tema utamanya adalah kenabian dan wahyu, bukti-bukti keesaan Allah di alam semesta, dialog dengan orang-orang kafir, serta kisah kaum Nabi Yunus yang selamat karena bertobat sebelum azab datang. Surah ini menegaskan bahwa Al-Qur'an adalah kitab yang penuh hikmah serta petunjuk dan rahmat bagi manusia.

Akidah: Keyakinan bahwa Al-Qur'an adalah wahyu Allah yang tidak dapat ditiru (ayat 37–38), Allah Mahakuasa atas segala sesuatu, dan hari kebangkitan adalah kepastian.
Ibadah: Berdoa dan memohon perlindungan hanya kepada Allah, bersyukur atas nikmat-Nya, serta menjaga salat dan zikir sebagai sarana mendekatkan diri kepada-Nya.
Akhlak: Tidak memaksa orang lain dalam beragama (ayat 99), bersabar menghadapi penolakan, dan bertawakal kepada Allah setelah berusaha.

3. Surah Hud (Hud) · Ayat 1–5

Awal Surah Hud dibuka dengan huruf alif laam raa dan penegasan bahwa Al-Qur'an adalah kitab yang ayat-ayatnya tersusun rapi lalu dijelaskan secara rinci. Nabi Muhammad diutus agar manusia tidak menyembah selain Allah, memohon ampunan, dan bertobat kepada-Nya, niscaya Allah memberi kenikmatan yang baik sampai waktu yang ditentukan. Allah mengetahui apa yang mereka sembunyikan dan yang mereka tampakkan.

"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."

Overview of Juz 11

Juz 11 contains At-Taubah 93-129, Surah Yunus in full, and Hud 1-5. The close of At-Taubah deals with the excuses of those who stayed behind from Tabuk, the Mosque of Harm, the repentance of the three who stayed behind, the call to study religion, and the Messenger's compassion. Yunus affirms the truth of revelation, Allah's signs in nature, freedom of faith, and the people of Jonah saved by repentance. The juz ends with the opening of Hud, calling to seek forgiveness and repent.

Juz begins: At-Taubah 9:93
Juz ends: Hud 11:5
Number of surahs: 3

1. Surah At-Taubah (The Repentance) · Verses 93–129

The close of At-Taubah describes the Bedouins and hypocrites who sought excuses to avoid Tabuk, while praising the first Emigrants and Ansar. Allah forbids praying in the Mosque of Harm built to divide, accepts the repentance of the three who stayed behind after the vast earth felt narrow to them, and commands some believers to study religion deeply. The surah ends with the Messenger's eagerness for his people's salvation and his compassion.

Thematic Division by Verses

VersesMain ThemeCore Message
93–99Hypocrites and Their HypocrisyAllah threatens hypocrites with punishment in this world and the Hereafter. They swear false oaths, fear exposure of their secrets, and prefer this world over the Hereafter.
100–118Virtues of the Early Generation and RepentancePraise for the Muhajirin and Ansar, and the story of the repentance of three companions who stayed behind from Tabuk (Ka'b bin Malik, Murarah bin ar-Rabi', Hilal bin Umayyah).
119–129Command to Fear Allah and ConclusionCommand to fear Allah, be with the truthful, prohibition of sin, and the concluding verse about a messenger from among themselves.

Messages of Creed, Worship, and Character

Aqidah (Creed): Allah accepts repentance and charity (9:104); Allah has purchased the believers' lives and wealth for Paradise (9:111).
Ibadah (Worship): Sincere repentance (9:117-118), charity to purify oneself (9:103), and studying religion deeply (9:122).
Akhlaq (Character): Being truthful and with the truthful (9:119), not dividing the community (9:107), and following the Messenger's compassion (9:128).

2. Surah Yunus (Jonas) · Verses 1–109

Meccan surah (109 verses). This surah was revealed near the end of the Meccan period. Main themes: prophethood, proofs of Allah’s oneness, dialogue with disbelievers, and the story of Prophet Jonah as a symbol of Allah’s mercy. It affirms that the Qur'an is a wise revelation and guidance for humanity.

Thematic Division by Verses

VersesMain ThemeCore Message
1–10Introduction: The Qur'an and Happiness of BelieversAffirmation that the Qur'an is a book of wisdom, glad tidings for believers, and warning for deniers.
11–20Swiftness of Punishment and Slowness of RevelationIf Allah hastened punishment as people hasten good, they would perish. Challenge to produce a similar surah.

61–80Story of Prophet Moses and Pharaoh and WarningAllah sent Moses with miracles to Pharaoh and his people. They continued to rebel, then were drowned.
81–100Triumph of Truth and Freedom of FaithAllah established the truth of Moses, saved the Children of Israel, and drowned Pharaoh and his people. Affirmation that faith cannot be forced.
101–109Command to Reflect on Creation and ConclusionCommand to observe the heavens and earth, warning for those who disbelieve, and command to rely on Allah and convey the message.

Messages of Creed, Worship, and Character

Aqidah (Creed): Belief that the Qur'an is Allah's inimitable revelation. Allah is All-Powerful, All-Forgiving, and Most Merciful. The Day of Resurrection is certain.
Ibadah (Worship): Command to pray and seek protection only from Allah. Maintaining balance between worldly and Hereafter affairs. Prayer and remembrance are means to draw closer to Allah.
Akhlaq (Character): Not forcing others in religion, being patient in the face of rejection, and always relying on Allah after making efforts. Emulating the patience of Prophet Jonah.

3. Surah Hud (Hud) · Verses 1–5

Hud opens with alif lam ra and the affirmation that the Qur'an is a Book whose verses are perfected and then explained in detail. The Prophet was sent so that people worship none but Allah, seek His forgiveness, and turn to Him in repentance, and He will grant them good enjoyment for an appointed term. Allah knows what they conceal and what they reveal.
