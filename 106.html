"Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pengasih, Maha Penyayang."
لِاِيْلٰفِ قُرَيْشٍۙ ﴿١﴾ اٖلٰفِهِمْ رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَاۤءِ وَالصَّيْفِۚ ﴿٢﴾ فَلْيَعْبُدُوْا رَبَّ هٰذَا الْبَيْتِۙ ﴿٣﴾ الَّذِيْٓ اَطْعَمَهُمْ مِّنْ جُوْعٍ ۙ وَّاٰمَنَهُمْ مِّنْ خَوْفٍ ࣖ ﴿٤﴾
Li'iilaafi quraisy (1) Iilaafihim rihlatasy-syitaa'i wash-shaif (2) Falya'buduu rabba haadzal-bait (3) Al-ladzii ath'amahum min juu'iw wa aamanahum min khauf (4)
"Karena kebiasaan orang-orang Quraisy, (1) (yaitu) kebiasaan mereka bepergian pada musim dingin dan musim panas. (2) Maka hendaklah mereka menyembah Tuhan (pemilik) rumah ini (Ka‘bah), (3) yang telah memberi makanan kepada mereka untuk menghilangkan lapar dan mengamankan mereka dari rasa ketakutan. (4)" (QS Quraisy [106]: 1–4)

Gambaran Umum Surah Quraisy (106)

Surah Al-Quraysh adalah surah ke-106 dalam Al-Qur'an yang terdiri atas 4 ayat pendek. Surah ini termasuk dalam kategori Makkiyah (diturunkan di Makkah) dan secara tematis merupakan kelanjutan dari Surah Al-Fil. Surah Al-Quraysh membahas tentang nikmat keamanan dan rezeki yang diberikan Allah kepada suku Quraisy, tanggung jawab untuk bersyukur dan beribadah, hubungan sebab-akibat antara nikmat dan kewajiban, serta pengingat akan perlindungan Allah yang berkelanjutan. Surah ini dimulai dengan menyebutkan kebiasaan suku Quraisy melakukan perjalanan dagang yang aman. Surah ini mengungkap prinsip bahwa setiap nikmat yang diberikan Allah membawa kewajiban tertentu, khususnya nikmat keamanan dan kemakmuran ekonomi.

Konteks Penurunan

Surah Quraisy diturunkan di Makkah dan berkaitan erat dengan Surah Al-Fil: setelah Allah melindungi Kakbah dari pasukan bergajah, kaum Quraisy dapat berdagang dengan aman. Surah ini mengingatkan mereka agar mensyukuri nikmat itu dengan menyembah Allah.

Tempat turun: Makkah (Makkiyah)
Periode: Periode awal Makkah
Latar belakang: Perjalanan dagang musim dingin dan musim panas
Tujuan: Mengajak mensyukuri nikmat keamanan dan rezeki dengan ibadah
Akidah: Keyakinan bahwa Allah-lah Pemberi keamanan dan rezeki, bukan kemampuan manusia semata. Nikmat yang berkelanjutan menunjukkan kasih sayang Allah yang harus disyukuri.
Ibadah: Ibadah yang diperintahkan adalah bentuk syukur atas nikmat. Salat dan ketaatan lainnya adalah wujud pengakuan bahwa segala kebaikan berasal dari Allah.
Akhlak: Menjauhi sifat lalai dan sombong; bersyukur dengan menggunakan nikmat untuk ketaatan; menjaga keamanan bersama sebagai amanah dari Allah.

Keutamaan Khusus Surah Quraisy

  • Prinsip Syukur atas Nikmat: Setiap nikmat mengharuskan syukur dan ibadah
  • Keamanan sebagai Nikmat Terbesar: Nikmat keamanan lebih berharga dari materi
  • Hubungan Ekonomi dan Ibadah: Kemakmuran ekonomi harus disertai kesadaran beribadah
  • Kontinuitas Perlindungan Allah: Perlindungan Allah berkelanjutan dari generasi ke generasi
  • Tanggung Jawab Kolektif: Nikmat kepada suatu kelompok membawa kewajiban kolektif

Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Mereka berniaga setiap tahun dengan aman dan sentosa. Karena itu, hendaklah mereka menyembah Tuhan pemilik rumah ini, yaitu Kakbah, dengan pengabdian yang tulus; Tuhan yang telah memberi mereka makanan untuk menghilangkan lapar dan mengamankan mereka dari rasa takut. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 3–4)

Pelajaran Spiritual dari Surah Quraisy

  • Akidah: Keyakinan bahwa Allah-lah Pemberi keamanan dan rezeki, bukan kemampuan manusia semata. Nikmat yang berkelanjutan menunjukkan kasih sayang Allah yang harus disyukuri.
  • Ibadah: Ibadah yang diperintahkan adalah bentuk syukur atas nikmat. Salat dan ketaatan lainnya adalah wujud pengakuan bahwa segala kebaikan berasal dari Allah.
  • Akhlak: Menjauhi sifat lalai dan sombong; bersyukur dengan menggunakan nikmat untuk ketaatan; menjaga keamanan bersama sebagai amanah dari Allah.

Hubungan dengan Surah Lain

  • Dengan Surah Al-Fil (105): Al-Fil menunjukkan perlindungan Allah dari ancaman eksternal, Al-Quraysh menunjukkan nikmat Allah dalam bentuk keamanan dan kemakmuran internal.
  • Dengan Surah Al-Ma'un (107): Al-Quraysh memerintahkan ibadah sebagai syukur nikmat, Al-Ma'un mengkritik orang yang salat tetapi lalai pada hak sosial.
  • Dengan Surah Ibrahim ayat 37: Keduanya membahas tentang doa Nabi Ibrahim untuk keamanan dan kecukupan rezeki penduduk Makkah.
  • Dengan Surah Al-Baqarah ayat 126: Keduanya menceritakan doa Nabi Ibrahim agar Makkah menjadi negeri yang aman dan diberi rezeki.
Sebelumnya: Al-Fil (105) Selanjutnya: Al-Ma'un (107)

Surah Quraisy (106) - Thematic Tafsir

Tafsir Source: IslamicStudies.info - Surah Quraisy Click Details  |  Ibn Kathir Tafsir (Quran.com)

Bismillāhir-raḥmānir-raḥīm
"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."
لِاِيْلٰفِ قُرَيْشٍۙ ﴿١﴾ اٖلٰفِهِمْ رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَاۤءِ وَالصَّيْفِۚ ﴿٢﴾ فَلْيَعْبُدُوْا رَبَّ هٰذَا الْبَيْتِۙ ﴿٣﴾ الَّذِيْٓ اَطْعَمَهُمْ مِّنْ جُوْعٍ ۙ وَّاٰمَنَهُمْ مِّنْ خَوْفٍ ࣖ ﴿٤﴾
Li’īlāfi quraish (1) Īlāfihim riḥlatash-shitā’i waṣ-ṣaif (2) Falya‘budū rabba hādhal-bait (3) Al-ladhī aṭ‘amahum min jū‘iw wa āmanahum min khauf (4)
"For the accustomed security of the Quraysh - (1) Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer - (2) Let them worship the Lord of this House, (3) Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear. (4)" (Quraisy 106:1–4)

Overview of Surah Quraisy (106)

Surah Al-Quraysh is the 106th chapter of the Qur'an consisting of 4 short verses. It is categorized as Makki (revealed in Mecca) and thematically continues from Surah Al-Fil. It deals with blessings of security and sustenance given by Allah to the Quraysh tribe, responsibility to be grateful and worship, cause-effect relationship between blessings and obligations, and reminder of Allah's continuous protection. This surah begins by mentioning the Quraysh tribe's custom of safe trade journeys. This surah reveals the principle that every blessing from Allah carries specific obligations, especially blessings of security and economic prosperity.

Revelation Context

Revealed in Mecca and closely tied to Al-Fil: after Allah protected the Ka'bah from the elephant army, the Quraysh could trade safely. The surah reminds them to thank Allah for this by worshipping Him.

Location: Mecca (Makki)
Period: Early Meccan period
Background: The winter and summer trade journeys
Purpose: Call to thank Allah for security and provision through worship
Aqidah (Creed): Belief that Allah alone is the Giver of security and sustenance, not human ability. Continuous blessings show Allah's mercy that must be thanked.
Ibadah (Worship): Worship commanded is a form of gratitude for blessings. Prayer and obedience acknowledge that all goodness comes from Allah.
Akhlaq (Character): Avoiding negligence and arrogance; being grateful by using blessings for obedience; maintaining collective security as a trust from Allah.

Special Significance of Surah Quraisy

  • Principle of Gratitude for Blessings: Every blessing requires gratitude and worship
  • Security as Greatest Blessing: Security is more valuable than material
  • Relationship Between Economy and Worship: Economic prosperity must be accompanied by worship awareness
  • Continuity of Allah's Protection: Allah's protection continues from generation to generation
  • Collective Responsibility: Blessings to a group bring collective obligations

Wisdom of Surah Quraisy

The Gift of Security

Security is the basis of prosperity.

The Gift of Provision

Allah feeds and removes hunger.

Gratitude through Worship

Blessings call for devotion to their Giver.

Honour of the Ka'bah

The Quraysh were honoured for tending the House.

Main Themes and Detailed Tafsir

1. Blessing of Security and Travel – Verses 1–2
لِاِيْلٰفِ قُرَيْشٍۙ ﴿١﴾ اٖلٰفِهِمْ رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَاۤءِ وَالصَّيْفِۚ ﴿٢﴾
Li’īlāfi quraish (1) Īlāfihim riḥlatash-shitā’i waṣ-ṣaif (2)
"For the accustomed security of the Quraysh - (1) Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer - (2)" (Quraisy 106:1–2)
Tafsir IslamicStudies.info: This verse contains several important lessons:
  1. Ilaaf: Custom or facility given by Allah for traveling safely
  2. Seasonal Journeys: To Yemen in winter and to Syria in summer for trade
  3. Absolute Security: Security guarantee that made them respected by all tribes
  4. Economic Prosperity: Ability to trade freely without fear of robbery or danger
This blessing was given after the Elephant Army event, showing continuity of Allah's protection from external threats to internal prosperity.

2. Obligation to Worship Lord of Ka'bah – Verse 3
فَلْيَعْبُدُوْا رَبَّ هٰذَا الْبَيْتِۙ ﴿٣﴾ الَّذِيْٓ اَطْعَمَهُمْ مِّنْ جُوْعٍ ۙ وَّاٰمَنَهُمْ مِّنْ خَوْفٍ ࣖ ﴿٤﴾
Falya‘budū rabba hādhal-bait (3) Al-ladhī aṭ‘amahum min jū‘iw wa āmanahum min khauf (4)
"Let them worship the Lord of this House, (3) Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear. (4)" (Quraisy 106:3–4)
Tafsir IslamicStudies.info: This worship command has important dimensions:
  1. Worship as Return for Blessings: Worship obligation emerges as consequence of blessings
  2. Pure Monotheism: Worship only Lord of Ka'bah, not idols around it
  3. Direct Relationship: Blessings from Allah, so worship only to Allah
  4. Acknowledgment of Reality: Acknowledging that all blessings come from Allah alone
  5. Special Responsibility: As Ka'bah guardians, their worship responsibility is greater
This verse is gentle admonition to Quraysh who, despite being Ka'bah guardians, worshipped idols.

Spiritual Lessons from Surah Quraisy

  • Aqidah (Creed): Belief that Allah alone is the Giver of security and sustenance, not human ability. Continuous blessings show Allah's mercy that must be thanked.
  • Ibadah (Worship): Worship commanded is a form of gratitude for blessings. Prayer and obedience acknowledge that all goodness comes from Allah.
  • Akhlaq (Character): Avoiding negligence and arrogance; being grateful by using blessings for obedience; maintaining collective security as a trust from Allah.

Relationship with Other Surahs

  • With Al-Fil (105): Al-Fil shows Allah's protection from external threats, Al-Quraysh shows Allah's blessings in form of internal security and prosperity.
  • With Al-Ma'un (107): Al-Quraysh commands worship as gratitude for blessings, Al-Ma'un criticizes those who pray but neglect social rights.
  • With Ibrahim verse 37: Both discuss Prophet Ibrahim's prayer for security and sufficient sustenance for Mecca inhabitants.
  • With Al-Baqarah verse 126: Both narrate Prophet Ibrahim's prayer for Mecca to become secure land given sustenance.
