Gambaran Umum Surah Al-Kautsar (108)

Surah Al-Kautsar adalah surah ke-108 dalam Al-Qur'an yang terdiri atas 3 ayat. Surah ini termasuk dalam kategori Makkiyah (diturunkan di Makkah) dan merupakan surah terpendek dalam Al-Qur'an. Surah Al-Kautsar diturunkan sebagai jawaban terhadap cemoohan orang-orang kafir Quraisy yang mengejek Nabi Muhammad saw. karena tidak memiliki keturunan laki-laki yang hidup. Surah ini mengandung tiga tema utama: pemberian nikmat Kautsar dari Allah, perintah untuk bersyukur melalui salat dan kurban, dan jaminan bahwa musuh Nabi akan terputus. Al-Kautsar bukan hanya merujuk pada sungai di surga, tetapi juga melambangkan segala bentuk kebaikan, nikmat, dan keberkahan yang Allah berikan kepada Nabi Muhammad saw. dan umatnya, termasuk Al-Qur'an, syafaat, telaga Kautsar di hari kiamat, dan banyaknya pengikut yang akan masuk surga.

Konteks Penurunan

Surah Al-Kautsar diturunkan di Makkah setelah wafatnya putra Nabi, Al-Qasim dan Abdullah, ketika Al-'Ash bin Wa'il mengejek beliau sebagai abtar (terputus keturunan). Anas meriwayatkan bahwa Nabi tersenyum ketika surah ini turun dan menjelaskan bahwa Al-Kautsar adalah sungai di surga yang dijanjikan Allah kepadanya (HR Muslim).

Tempat turun: Makkah (Makkiyah)
Periode: Periode awal Makkah
Latar belakang: Ejekan bahwa Nabi terputus keturunan
Tujuan: Menghibur Nabi dan menegaskan nikmat yang berlimpah
Akidah: Keyakinan bahwa Allah-lah Pemberi segala nikmat, dan Dia akan membela hamba-Nya yang bersabar. Kemenangan akhir adalah milik orang beriman.
Ibadah: Salat dan kurban adalah wujud syukur atas nikmat. Ibadah harus dilakukan dengan ikhlas hanya karena Allah (“lirabbika”).
Akhlak: Menghadapi ejekan dengan kesabaran, tidak membalas dengan keburukan, serta membuktikan kebaikan melalui amal saleh. Menjauhi sifat iri dan dengki seperti musuh Nabi.

Keutamaan Khusus Surah Al-Kautsar

  • Penghiburan untuk Nabi: Menghibur Nabi saw. atas cemoohan orang kafir tentang keturunan
  • Janji Kemenangan: Janji Allah bahwa musuh Islam akan terputus bukan pengikut Nabi
  • Bentuk Syukur yang Benar: Syukur melalui ibadah ritual (salat) dan sosial (kurban)
  • Nama-nama Allah: Menggunakan nama "Rabbika" (Tuhanmu) yang menunjukkan hubungan personal
  • Konsep Kautsar: Nikmat yang melimpah mencakup dunia dan akhirat

Ikhlas

Kebencian Merugikan Diri

Pembenci kebenaranlah yang sebenarnya terputus.

Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Sebagai rasa syukur kepada Tuhanmu, laksanakanlah salat dengan ikhlas semata-mata karena Allah, bukan karena ria, dan berkurbanlah demi mendekatkan diri kepada-Nya serta berbagi dengan sesama. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 2)
3. Musuh Nabi yang Terputus – Ayat 3

Inna syaani'aka huwal-abtar
"Sungguh, orang-orang yang membencimu dialah yang terputus (dari rahmat Allah)." (QS Al-Kautsar [108]: 3)
Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Sungguh, orang-orang yang membencimu dan mengabaikan hidayah yang engkau bawa, dialah yang terputus; bukan hanya terputus jejak dan keturunannya, tetapi juga dijauhkan dari rahmat Allah. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 3)

Pelajaran Spiritual dari Surah Al-Kautsar

  • Akidah: Keyakinan bahwa Allah-lah Pemberi segala nikmat, dan Dia akan membela hamba-Nya yang bersabar. Kemenangan akhir adalah milik orang beriman.
  • Ibadah: Salat dan kurban adalah wujud syukur atas nikmat. Ibadah harus dilakukan dengan ikhlas hanya karena Allah (“lirabbika”).
  • Akhlak: Menghadapi ejekan dengan kesabaran, tidak membalas dengan keburukan, serta membuktikan kebaikan melalui amal saleh. Menjauhi sifat iri dan dengki seperti musuh Nabi.

Hubungan dengan Surah Lain

  • Dengan Surah Al-Ma'un (107): Al-Ma'un mengkritik orang yang salat tapi lalai, Al-Kautsar memerintahkan salat dengan ikhlas untuk Allah.
  • Dengan Surah Al-Kafirun (109): Al-Kautsar berbicara tentang nikmat untuk Nabi, Al-Kafirun tentang pemisahan antara iman dan kufur.
  • Dengan Surah Ad-Dhuha (93): Keduanya menghibur Nabi saw. dan menjanjikan nikmat di kemudian hari sebagai ganti kesulitan awal.
  • Dengan Surah An-Nashr (110): Keduanya berbicara tentang kemenangan Islam dan perintah untuk bersyukur kepada Allah.

Surah Al-Kautsar (108) - Thematic Tafsir

Tafsir Source: IslamicStudies.info - Surah Al-Kautsar Click Details  |  Ibn Kathir Tafsir (Quran.com)  |  Qur'an Kemenag
بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ
Bismillāhir-raḥmānir-raḥīm
"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."
اِنَّآ اَعْطَيْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَۗ ﴿١﴾ فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَانْحَرْۗ ﴿٢﴾ اِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْاَبْتَرُ ࣖ ﴿٣﴾
Innā a‘ṭainākal-kauthar (1) Faṣalli lirabbika wanḥar (2) Inna shāni’aka huwal-abtar (3)
"Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], al-Kawthar. (1) So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone]. (2) Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off. (3)" (Al-Kautsar 108:1–3)

Overview of Surah Al-Kautsar (108)

Surah Al-Kautsar is the 108th chapter of the Qur'an consisting of 3 verses. It is categorized as Makki (revealed in Mecca) and is the shortest surah in the Qur'an. Surah Al-Kautsar was revealed as response to the mockery of the Quraysh disbelievers who mocked Prophet Muhammad ﷺ for not having surviving male children. This surah contains three main themes: granting of Al-Kawthar from Allah, command to give thanks through prayer and sacrifice, and assurance that the Prophet's enemy will be cut off. Al-Kawthar refers not only to a river in Paradise but also symbolizes all forms of goodness, blessings, and abundance that Allah has given to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his ummah, including the Qur'an, intercession, the Kawthar pond on Judgment Day, and the multitude of followers who will enter Paradise.

Revelation Context

Revealed in Mecca after the death of the Prophet's sons al-Qasim and 'Abdullah, when al-'As ibn Wa'il mocked him as abtar (cut off from posterity). Anas reported that the Prophet smiled when it was revealed and explained that al-Kawthar is a river in Paradise promised to him (Muslim).

Location: Mecca (Makki)
Period: Early Meccan period
Background: Mockery that the Prophet was cut off
Purpose: Console the Prophet and affirm abundant blessing
Aqidah (Creed): Belief that Allah alone is the Giver of all blessings, and He will defend His patient servant. Ultimate victory belongs to the believers.
Ibadah (Worship): Prayer and sacrifice are forms of gratitude for blessings. Worship must be performed sincerely only for Allah (“lirabbika”).
Akhlaq (Character): Facing mockery with patience, not retaliating with evil, and proving goodness through righteous deeds. Avoiding envy and hatred like the enemies of the Prophet.

Translation: "Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], al-Kawthar. (1) So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone]. (2) Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off. (3)" (Al-Kautsar 108:1–3)
Tafsir Summary (Kemenag): Allah gave the Prophet abundant and lasting blessings, including the pond of al-Kawthar. In gratitude, pray sincerely and sacrifice for Allah. Indeed, the one who hates you and ignores your guidance is the one cut off from Allah's mercy. 📖 Read the Kemenag tafsir

Thematic Division by Verses

VersesMain ThemeCore Message
1Granting of KawtharAllah announces the granting of abundant Kawthar to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

3Fate of Prophet's EnemyDeclaration that the enemy who hates the Prophet ﷺ will be cut off and devoid of blessings.

Special Significance of Surah Al-Kautsar

  • Consolation for the Prophet: Consoling the Prophet ﷺ over disbelievers' mockery about offspring
  • Promise of Victory: Allah's promise that enemies of Islam will be cut off, not the Prophet's followers
  • Correct Form of Gratitude: Gratitude through ritual worship (prayer) and social worship (sacrifice)
  • Allah's Names: Use of "Rabbika" (your Lord) indicating personal relationship
  • Concept of Kawthar: Abundant blessings encompassing both worldly and hereafter

Wisdom of Surah Al-Kautsar

Abundant Blessing

Allah gave the Prophet boundless good.

Gratitude through Worship

Prayer and sacrifice express thanks.

Sincerity

Worship is for Allah alone.

Hatred Harms the Hater

The enemy of truth is the one truly cut off.

Main Themes and Detailed Tafsir

1. Abundant Blessing of Kawthar – Verse 1
اِنَّآ اَعْطَيْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَۗ ﴿١﴾
Innā a‘ṭainākal-kauthar
"Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muhammad], al-Kawthar." (Al-Kautsar 108:1)
Tafsir IslamicStudies.info: According to IslamicStudies.info, “Innā” (Indeed We) indicates certainty and strength of Allah's promise. “A‘ṭaināka” (We have granted you) in past tense affirms the certainty of the gift. Al-Kawthar is abundant and continuous blessings, including the pond in Paradise, intercession, the Qur’an, and numerous followers. This verse was revealed in response to Al-‘As ibn Wa’il who mocked the Prophet as “abtar” (cut off).
📖 Read full tafsir
2. Command for Prayer and Sacrifice – Verse 2
فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَانْحَرْۗ ﴿٢﴾
Faṣalli lirabbika wanḥar
"So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone]." (Al-Kautsar 108:2)
Tafsir IslamicStudies.info: The particle “Fa” indicates the logical consequence of the blessing given. “Shalli lirabbika” (pray to your Lord) emphasizes sincerity in ritual worship. “Wanhar” (and sacrifice) is social worship involving wealth sacrifice. Together they represent total devotion: heart and wealth. This command teaches that gratitude for blessings must be expressed through worship.
📖 Read full tafsir
3. Prophet's Enemy Who is Cut Off – Verse 3
اِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْاَبْتَرُ ࣖ ﴿٣﴾
Inna shāni’aka huwal-abtar
"Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off." (Al-Kautsar 108:3)
Tafsir IslamicStudies.info: “Syaani’aka” (your enemy) refers specifically to Al-‘As ibn Wa’il and other enemies of the Prophet. “Al-Abtar” (the cut off) is the same term they used to mock the Prophet, but Allah turns it back on them. They are cut off from lineage, from worldly good, and from Hereafter mercy. Historical proof: the descendants of the Prophet’s enemies perished, while the Prophet’s descendants through Fatimah continue until today.

Spiritual Lessons from Surah Al-Kautsar

  • Aqidah (Creed): Belief that Allah alone is the Giver of all blessings, and He will defend His patient servant. Ultimate victory belongs to the believers.
  • Ibadah (Worship): Prayer and sacrifice are forms of gratitude for blessings. Worship must be performed sincerely only for Allah (“lirabbika”).
  • Akhlaq (Character): Facing mockery with patience, not retaliating with evil, and proving goodness through righteous deeds. Avoiding envy and hatred like the enemies of the Prophet.

Relationship with Other Surahs

  • With Al-Ma'un (107): Al-Ma'un criticizes those who pray but are negligent, Al-Kautsar commands prayer with sincerity for Allah.
  • With Al-Kafirun (109): Al-Kautsar speaks about blessings for the Prophet, Al-Kafirun about separation between faith and disbelief.
  • With Ad-Dhuha (93): Both console the Prophet ﷺ and promise later blessings in exchange for early difficulties.
  • With An-Nashr (110): Both speak about Islam's victory and command to give thanks to Allah.
