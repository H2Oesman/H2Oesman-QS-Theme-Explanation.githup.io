بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ
Bismillaahir-rahmaanir-rahiim
"Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pengasih, Maha Penyayang."

Gambaran Umum Juz 17

Juz 17 memuat Surah Al-Anbiya' dan Surah Al-Hajj secara lengkap. Surah Al-Anbiya' menegaskan dekatnya hari perhitungan dan keesaan Allah, lalu mengisahkan banyak nabi, seperti Ibrahim, Luth, Nuh, Daud, Sulaiman, Ayyub, Yunus, Zakaria, dan Isa, beserta doa-doa mereka. Surah Al-Hajj menggambarkan dahsyatnya kiamat, proses penciptaan manusia sebagai bukti kebangkitan, panggilan haji Nabi Ibrahim, syiar kurban, dan izin berperang bagi orang yang dizalimi.

Halaman: 322–341
Awal juz: QS Al-Anbiya' [21]: 1
Akhir juz: QS Al-Hajj [22]: 78
Jumlah surah: 2

Ayat Pilihan Juz 17

وَذَا النُّوْنِ اِذْ ذَّهَبَ مُغَاضِبًا فَظَنَّ اَنْ لَّنْ نَّقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ فَنَادٰى فِى الظُّلُمٰتِ اَنْ لَّآ اِلٰهَ اِلَّآ اَنْتَ سُبْحٰنَكَ اِنِّيْ كُنْتُ مِنَ الظّٰلِمِيْنَ ۚ ﴿٨٧﴾
Wa dzan-nuuni idz dzahaba mughaadhiban fazhanna al lan naqdira 'alaihi fanaadaa fizh-zhulumaati al laa ilaaha illaa anta subhaanaka innii kuntu minazh-zhaalimiin
Terjemahan: "Dan (ingatlah kisah) Zun Nun (Yunus), ketika dia pergi dalam keadaan marah, lalu dia menyangka bahwa Kami tidak akan menyulitkannya, maka dia berdoa dalam keadaan yang sangat gelap, ”Tidak ada tuhan selain Engkau, Mahasuci Engkau. Sungguh, aku termasuk orang-orang yang zalim.”" (QS Al-Anbiya' [21]: 87)
Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Ingatlah kisah Zun Nun (Yunus) ketika ia pergi meninggalkan kaumnya dalam keadaan marah dan menyangka bahwa Allah tidak akan menyulitkannya. Lalu dalam kegelapan perut ikan ia berdoa, "Tidak ada tuhan selain Engkau, Mahasuci Engkau, sungguh aku termasuk orang-orang yang zalim." Doa ini menjadi teladan pengakuan dosa dan tobat dalam kesulitan. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 87)

1. Surah Al-Anbiya' (Para Nabi) · Ayat 1–112

Surah ke-21 (112 ayat) Makkiyah. Dinamai "Al-Anbiya'" (para nabi) karena memuat kisah banyak nabi. Surah ini turun sebagai bantahan terhadap kaum musyrik Makkah yang meragukan kerasulan Nabi Muhammad dan hari kebangkitan. Surah ini menegaskan tauhid, kebenaran wahyu, serta konsistensi risalah para nabi. Juga menampilkan bukti-bukti kekuasaan Allah di alam semesta.

Pesan Akidah, Ibadah, dan Akhlak

Akidah: Kedekatan hari kiamat (21:1), keesaan Allah (21:22), kebangkitan setelah mati (21:47), dan bahwa Al-Qur'an adalah kebenaran mutlak.
Ibadah: Berdoa dengan harap dan takut (21:90), bertasbih siang malam (21:20), bersyukur, dan mengikuti petunjuk para nabi.
Akhlak: Berpikir kritis (Ibrahim), sabar (Ayyub), adil (Daud), rendah hati (Sulaiman), lembut dalam dakwah, dan tidak taklid buta.

2. Surah Al-Hajj (Haji) · Ayat 1–78

Surah ke-22 (78 ayat) Madaniyah. Dinamai "Al-Hajj" karena mengandung hukum dan hikmah ibadah haji. Surah ini menggabungkan peringatan dahsyatnya hari Kiamat, tata cara haji, izin berperang untuk membela diri, serta prinsip bahwa amal yang diterima didasari ketakwaan. Surah ini menjadi pedoman penting bagi umat Islam dalam menjalankan rukun Islam kelima.

Pesan Akidah, Ibadah, dan Akhlak

Akidah: Kedahsyatan hari kiamat (22:1–2), kebangkitan setelah mati (22:5–7), keesaan Allah (22:62), janji pertolongan-Nya (22:40).
Ibadah: Kewajiban haji (22:27–30), kurban (22:36–37), salat, zikir, doa, istigfar.
Akhlak: Takwa, sabar, membela kebenaran, menghormati syiar Allah, keadilan, tidak melampaui batas.

"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."

Overview of Juz 17

Juz 17 contains Surahs Al-Anbiya' and Al-Hajj in full. Al-Anbiya' affirms the nearness of the reckoning and Allah's oneness, then recounts many prophets, Abraham, Lot, Noah, David, Solomon, Job, Jonah, Zechariah, and Jesus, and their prayers. Al-Hajj depicts the terror of the Hour, human creation as proof of resurrection, Abraham's call to pilgrimage, the rites of sacrifice, and permission to fight for those wronged.

Pages: 322–341
Juz begins: Al-Anbiya' 21:1
Juz ends: Al-Hajj 22:78
Number of surahs: 2

Selected Verse of Juz 17

وَذَا النُّوْنِ اِذْ ذَّهَبَ مُغَاضِبًا فَظَنَّ اَنْ لَّنْ نَّقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ فَنَادٰى فِى الظُّلُمٰتِ اَنْ لَّآ اِلٰهَ اِلَّآ اَنْتَ سُبْحٰنَكَ اِنِّيْ كُنْتُ مِنَ الظّٰلِمِيْنَ ۚ ﴿٨٧﴾
Wa dhan-nūni idh dhahaba mughāḍiban faẓanna al lan naqdira ‘alaihi fanādā fiẓ-ẓulumāti al lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka innī kuntu minaẓ-ẓālimīn
Translation: "And [mention] the man of the fish, when he went off in anger and thought that We would not decree [anything] upon him. And he called out within the darknesses, "There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers."" (Al-Anbiya' 21:87)
Tafsir Summary: Remember Dhun-Nun (Jonah), when he left his people in anger, thinking Allah would not constrain him. Then in the darkness of the fish he called out, "There is no god but You; glory be to You; I have been among the wrongdoers." The prayer is a model of confession and repentance in hardship. 📖 Read Ibn Kathir tafsir (Verse 87)

1. Surah Al-Anbiya' (The Prophets) · Verses 1–112

Meccan surah (112 verses) named "The Prophets". It refutes the polytheists' denial of prophethood and resurrection, affirms tawhid, and presents stories of numerous prophets to show the consistency of the divine message. It also presents scientific signs of Allah's power.

11–29Refutation of ShirkFalsehood of idols, oneness of Allah, creation.

51–70Story of Ibrahim & Destruction of IdolsIbrahim breaks idols, is thrown into fire, saved by Allah.
71–82Stories of Lut, Nuh, Dawud, SulaymanSalvation of Lut and Nuh, power of Dawud and Sulayman, subjugation of wind and jinn.
83–94Stories of Ayyub, Yunus, Zakariya, IsaPatience of Ayyub, Yunus's prayer in the whale, Zakariya's prayer answered, birth of Yahya and Isa.
95–112Conclusion & Call to TawhidCertainty of resurrection, Prophet Muhammad as mercy, call to worship One God.

Messages of Creed, Worship, and Character

Aqidah (Creed): Nearness of the Hour (21:1), oneness of Allah (21:22), resurrection after death (21:47), and the Qur'an as absolute truth.
Ibadah (Worship): Supplicate with hope and fear (21:90), glorify Allah day and night (21:20), be grateful, and follow the guidance of the prophets.
Akhlaq (Character): Critical thinking (Ibrahim), patience (Ayyub), justice (Dawud), humility (Sulayman), gentleness in da'wah, and rejecting blind following.

2. Surah Al-Hajj (The Pilgrimage) · Verses 1–78

Madinan surah (78 verses) named after the pilgrimage. It combines warnings of the Last Day, laws of Hajj, permission for self-defense, and the essence of accepted worship. It serves as a comprehensive guide for the fifth pillar of Islam.

Messages of Creed, Worship, and Character

Aqidah (Creed): Terror of the Last Day (22:1-2), resurrection after death (22:5-7), oneness of Allah (22:62), promise of divine help (22:40).
Ibadah (Worship): Obligation of Hajj (22:27-30), sacrifice (22:36-37), prayer, dhikr, supplication, seeking forgiveness.
Akhlaq (Character): Taqwa, patience, defending truth, honoring Allah's symbols, justice, not transgressing limits.
