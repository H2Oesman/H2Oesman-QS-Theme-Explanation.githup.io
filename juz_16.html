Gambaran Umum Juz 16

Juz 16 memuat Surah Al-Kahf ayat 75–110, Surah Maryam, dan Surah Thaha secara lengkap. Kisah Musa dan Khidir berlanjut hingga terungkap hikmah di balik peristiwa-peristiwa yang tampak aneh, disusul kisah Zulkarnain dan penutup Al-Kahf tentang amal saleh dan tidak menyekutukan Allah. Surah Maryam mengisahkan Nabi Zakaria, Yahya, Maryam, kelahiran Isa, dan dakwah Nabi Ibrahim kepada ayahnya. Surah Thaha mengisahkan Nabi Musa secara panjang dan doa memohon tambahan ilmu.

Halaman: 302–321
Awal juz: QS Al-Kahf [18]: 75
Akhir juz: QS Thaha [20]: 135
Jumlah surah: 3

Ayat Pilihan Juz 16

فَتَعٰلَى اللّٰهُ الْمَلِكُ الْحَقُّۚ وَلَا تَعْجَلْ بِالْقُرْاٰنِ مِنْ قَبْلِ اَنْ يُّقْضٰٓى اِلَيْكَ وَحْيُهٗ ۖوَقُلْ رَّبِّ زِدْنِيْ عِلْمًا ﴿١١٤﴾
Fata'aalallaahul-malikul-haqq, wa laa ta'jal bil-qur'aani min qabli ay yuqdhaa ilaika wahyuh, wa qur rabbi zidnii 'ilmaa
Terjemahan: "Maka Mahatinggi Allah, Raja yang sebenar-benarnya. Dan janganlah engkau (Muhammad) tergesa-gesa (membaca) Al-Qur'an sebelum selesai diwahyukan kepadamu, dan katakanlah, “Ya Tuhanku, tambahkanlah ilmu kepadaku. ”" (QS Thaha [20]: 114)
Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Mahatinggi Allah, Raja yang sebenar-benarnya. Janganlah engkau, wahai Nabi Muhammad, tergesa-gesa membaca Al-Qur'an sebelum selesai diwahyukan kepadamu, agar tidak keliru memahami dan mengajarkannya, dan katakanlah, "Ya Tuhanku, tambahkanlah kepadaku ilmu pengetahuan yang bermanfaat." 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 114)

1. Surah Al-Kahf (Goa) · Ayat 75–110

Bagian akhir Surah Al-Kahf melanjutkan perjalanan Nabi Musa bersama Khidir hingga Khidir menjelaskan hikmah di balik melubangi perahu, membunuh seorang anak, dan menegakkan dinding yang hampir roboh. Selanjutnya dikisahkan Zulkarnain, penguasa saleh yang membangun tembok penghalang Ya'juj dan Ma'juj. Surah ditutup dengan peringatan tentang orang yang paling rugi amalnya, luasnya ilmu Allah, dan perintah beramal saleh serta tidak menyekutukan Allah dalam ibadah.

Pesan Akidah, Ibadah, dan Akhlak

Akidah: Di balik takdir Allah selalu ada hikmah (ayat 78–82); ilmu Allah tidak akan habis meskipun lautan menjadi tintanya (ayat 109).
Ibadah: Beramal saleh dan tidak menyekutukan Allah dalam ibadah (ayat 110).
Akhlak: Sabar dan rendah hati dalam menuntut ilmu, adil dalam berkuasa seperti Zulkarnain (ayat 87–88), dan menyandarkan keberhasilan kepada rahmat Allah (ayat 98).

2. Surah Maryam (Maryam) · Ayat 1–98

Surah ke-19 (98 ayat) Makkiyah. Dinamai Maryam karena menceritakan kisah Maryam dan kelahiran Nabi Isa. Surah ini diturunkan sebagai hiburan bagi Nabi Muhammad dan umat Islam di Makkah. Tema utamanya: rahmat Allah, mukjizat kelahiran Isa, bantahan terhadap anggapan Allah memiliki anak, serta kisah para nabi yang menunjukkan keutamaan tauhid dan kesabaran.

Pesan Akidah, Ibadah, dan Akhlak

Akidah: Keesaan Allah, penolakan syirik, kekuasaan mutlak Allah mencipta tanpa sebab. Isa adalah hamba dan rasul Allah, bukan anak Tuhan. (19:34–36, 19:88–95)
Ibadah: Berdoa dengan khusyuk (seperti Zakaria), bersyukur atas nikmat, membaca Al-Qur'an dengan tartil, dan meneladani kesalehan para nabi. (19:2–6, 19:12–13)
Akhlak: Berbakti kepada orang tua (Ibrahim), menjaga kesucian (Maryam), kesabaran dalam berdakwah, dan tidak putus asa dari rahmat Allah. (19:41–48, 19:16–28)

3. Surah Thaha (Taha) · Ayat 1–135

Surah ke-20 (135 ayat) Makkiyah. Dinamai dari huruf pembuka "Thaha". Surah ini turun untuk menghibur Nabi Muhammad saw. dari tekanan kaum musyrik, berisi kisah detail Nabi Musa, penegasan tauhid, perintah salat, dan peringatan hari kiamat. Surah ini juga dikenal karena doa Nabi Musa yang sangat masyhur.

Akidah: Keesaan Allah (20:14), kenabian Musa dan Muhammad, keniscayaan hari kiamat (20:102–104), dan bahwa Allah tidak memiliki anak. (20:116)
Ibadah: Mendirikan salat dengan khusyuk (20:14), berdoa dengan doa-doa yang diajarkan Al-Qur'an (20:25–32), membaca Al-Qur'an dengan tartil.
Akhlak: Berkata lembut (20:44), sabar dalam menghadapi ujian, rendah hati, tolong-menolong dalam kebaikan (Musa dan Harun).

"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."

Overview of Juz 16

Juz 16 contains Al-Kahf 75-110 and Surahs Maryam and Taha in full. Moses' journey with al-Khidr continues until the wisdom behind the puzzling events is revealed, followed by Dhul-Qarnayn and the close of Al-Kahf on righteous deeds and pure worship. Maryam tells of Zechariah, John, Mary, the birth of Jesus, and Abraham's call to his father. Taha recounts Moses at length and the prayer for increased knowledge.

Pages: 302–321
Juz begins: Al-Kahf 18:75
Juz ends: Thaha 20:135
Number of surahs: 3

Juz divisions and pages follow the standard Madinah Mushaf.

Translation: "So high [above all] is Allah, the Sovereign, the Truth. And, [O Muhammad], do not hasten with [recitation of] the Qur'an before its revelation is completed to you, and say, "My Lord, increase me in knowledge."" (Thaha 20:114)
Tafsir Summary (Kemenag): Exalted is Allah, the True King. O Prophet, do not hasten to recite the Qur'an before its revelation to you is complete, lest you misunderstand or mis-teach it, and say, "My Lord, increase me in beneficial knowledge." 📖 Read the Kemenag tafsir (Verses 114)

1. Surah Al-Kahf (The Cave) · Verses 75–110

The close of Al-Kahf continues Moses' journey with al-Khidr until al-Khidr explains the wisdom behind holing the boat, killing the boy, and repairing the falling wall. Then comes Dhul-Qarnayn, a righteous ruler who built the barrier against Gog and Magog. The surah ends warning of those whose deeds are utterly lost, the vastness of Allah's knowledge, and the command to do good and associate none in worship.

Thematic Division by Verses

83–98Story of Dhul-Qarnayn & Ya'juj Ma'jujJust ruler builds an iron barrier; signs of the Hour.
99–110Resurrection & conclusionThe Hour will surely come; good deeds and tawhid as provision.

Messages of Creed, Worship, and Character

Aqidah (Creed): Behind Allah's decree there is always wisdom (18:78-82); His words would not run out even if the sea were ink (18:109).
Ibadah (Worship): Doing righteous deeds and associating none in worship (18:110).
Akhlaq (Character): Patience and humility in learning, justice in power like Dhul-Qarnayn (18:87-88), and attributing success to Allah's mercy (18:98).

2. Surah Maryam (Mary) · Verses 1–98

Meccan surah (98 verses) named after Maryam (Mary). It was revealed to console the Prophet and Muslims facing persecution in Mecca. Main themes: Allah's mercy, the miraculous birth of Isa, refutation of the claim that Allah has a son, and stories of prophets showing the virtue of Tawheed and patience.

76–87Reward for the righteous & warning for deniersAllah increases guidance for those who accept; good deeds are everlasting; wrongdoers will perish.
88–98Rejection of the claim "Allah has a son" & conclusionThe claim that Allah has a son is a grave falsehood; all creatures will come as servants; believers who do good deeds will be loved.

Messages of Creed, Worship, and Character

Aqidah (Creed): Absolute Tawheed, rejection of shirk, Allah's power to create without cause. Isa is a servant and messenger, not God's son. (19:34-36, 19:88-95)
Ibadah (Worship): Pray with humility (like Zakariya), be grateful, recite the Qur'an with reflection, emulate the piety of the prophets. (19:2-6, 19:12-13)
Akhlaq (Character): Honor parents (Ibrahim), maintain chastity (Maryam), be patient in da'wah, never despair of Allah's mercy. (19:41-48, 19:16-28)

3. Surah Thaha (Taa-Haa) · Verses 1–135

Meccan surah (135 verses) named after the opening letters "Ṭāhā". It was revealed to console the Prophet Muhammad during difficult times in Mecca. It contains the detailed story of Prophet Musa, affirmation of Tawhid, the command to establish prayer, and warnings of the Last Day. The surah is also famous for Musa's comprehensive supplication.

9–24Prophetic Call of Musa in Ṭuwā ValleyMusa sees fire, is called by Allah, commanded to remove sandals, given miracles of the staff and shining hand.

90–104Aftermath of Idolatry & the Last DayMusa confronts Samiri. Terrifying description of the Day of Judgment: sinners with blue eyes.
105–115The Final Hour and the Qur'anMountains scattered, earth flattened. The Qur'an is a clear reminder in Arabic. Prophet told to pray for more knowledge.
116–135Story of Adam & ConclusionIblis refuses to prostrate, Adam tempted. Allah's guidance saves, misguidance leads to ruin. Command to be patient and pray.

Messages of Creed, Worship, and Character

Aqidah (Creed): Oneness of Allah (20:14), prophethood of Musa and Muhammad, certainty of the Last Day (20:102-104), and that Allah has no son. (20:116)
Ibadah (Worship): Establish prayer with devotion (20:14), supplicate with Qur'anic prayers (20:25-32), recite the Qur'an with care.
Akhlaq (Character): Speak gently (20:44), be patient, humble, cooperate in goodness (Musa and Harun).
