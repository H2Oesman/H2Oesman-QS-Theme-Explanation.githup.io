Surah Asy-Syarh (94) - Tafsir Tematik

Bismillaahir-rahmaanir-rahiim
"Dengan nama Allah Yang Maha Pengasih, Maha Penyayang."
اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَۙ ﴿١﴾ وَوَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَۙ ﴿٢﴾ الَّذِيْٓ اَنْقَضَ ظَهْرَكَۙ ﴿٣﴾ وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَۗ ﴿٤﴾
Alam nasyrah laka shadrak (1) Wa wadha'naa 'anka wizrak (2) Al-ladzii anqadha zhahrak (3) Wa rafa'naa laka dzikrak (4)
"Bukankah Kami telah melapangkan dadamu (Muhammad)? (1) dan Kami pun telah menurunkan bebanmu darimu, (2) yang memberatkan punggungmu, (3) dan Kami tinggikan sebutan (nama)mu bagimu. (4)" (QS Asy-Syarh [94]: 1–4)

Gambaran Umum Surah Asy-Syarh (94)

Surah Al-Insyirah, juga dikenal sebagai Ash-Sharh, adalah surah ke-94 dalam Al-Qur'an yang terdiri atas 8 ayat. Surah ini termasuk dalam kategori Makkiyah (diturunkan di Makkah) dan merupakan surah pendek yang sangat bermakna. Nama "Al-Insyirah" berarti "Melapangkan" atau "Kelonggaran" yang merujuk pada pembukaan dada Nabi Muhammad. Surah Al-Insyirah secara tematis membahas tentang pelapangan dada Nabi Muhammad, pengangkatan beban berat darinya, prinsip kesulitan dan kemudahan yang berpasangan, dan anjuran untuk beribadah setelah kesulitan berlalu. Surah ini diturunkan sebagai kelanjutan dari penghiburan dalam Surah Ad-Dhuha, memberikan jaminan bahwa setiap kesulitan pasti diikuti kemudahan. Surah ini merupakan respons ilahi terhadap beban berat kenabian, dengan menegaskan bahwa Allah telah memberikan kekuatan dan kemudahan kepada Nabi untuk menjalankan tugasnya.

Konteks Penurunan

Surah Al-Insyirah diturunkan di Makkah setelah Surah Adh-Dhuha, pada masa Nabi menghadapi tekanan berat dalam berdakwah. Kedua surah ini saling melengkapi dalam menghibur dan meneguhkan hati Nabi.

Tempat turun: Makkah (Makkiyah)
Periode: Periode awal Makkah
Latar belakang: Beratnya beban dakwah
Tujuan: Meneguhkan hati Nabi dan menanamkan optimisme
Akidah: Keyakinan bahwa Allah Maha Meringankan, setiap kesulitan pasti ada kemudahan.
Ibadah: Salat dan ibadah sebagai bentuk syukur setelah selesai dari urusan dunia.
Akhlak: Keseimbangan antara kerja keras dan tawakal, serta optimisme dalam menghadapi ujian.

Keutamaan Khusus Surah Asy-Syarh

  • Prinsip Universal: Setiap kesulitan pasti diiringi kemudahan
  • Kekuatan Spiritual: Allah memberikan kekuatan internal untuk menghadapi tantangan
  • Transformasi Diri: Dari dada yang sempit menjadi lapang menerima hidayah
  • Keseimbangan Hidup: Siklus kesulitan dan kemudahan sebagai sunatullah
  • Orientasi Ibadah: Ibadah sebagai respons terhadap nikmat Allah

Hikmah Surah Asy-Syarh

Lapang Dada

Kelapangan dada adalah nikmat terbesar bagi pengemban amanah.

Kemudahan Menyertai Kesulitan

Setiap kesulitan selalu disertai kemudahan.

Produktif dan Tidak Berhenti

Selesai satu urusan, lanjutkan dengan urusan lain.

Berharap Hanya kepada Allah

Tawakal adalah sandaran setelah berusaha.

Tema Utama dan Tafsir Rinci

1. Pelapangan Dada & Pengangkatan Beban (1–4)
اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَۙ ﴿١﴾ وَوَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَۙ ﴿٢﴾ الَّذِيْٓ اَنْقَضَ ظَهْرَكَۙ ﴿٣﴾ وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَۗ ﴿٤﴾
Alam nasyrah laka shadrak (1) Wa wadha'naa 'anka wizrak (2) Al-ladzii anqadha zhahrak (3) Wa rafa'naa laka dzikrak (4)
"Bukankah Kami telah melapangkan dadamu (Muhammad)? (1) dan Kami pun telah menurunkan bebanmu darimu, (2) yang memberatkan punggungmu, (3) dan Kami tinggikan sebutan (nama)mu bagimu. (4)" (QS Asy-Syarh [94]: 1–4)
Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Wahai Nabi, bukankah Allah telah melapangkan dadamu, menjadikanmu nabi yang berakhlak mulia, berwawasan luas, dan sabar? Allah telah menurunkan bebanmu yang memberatkan punggungmu dengan menjadikan tugas risalah terasa ringan, dan meninggikan sebutan namamu dengan menyebutnya bersama nama-Nya dalam syahadat, azan, dan tasyahud. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 1–4)
2. Prinsip Kesulitan dan Kemudahan (5–6)
فَاِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًاۙ ﴿٥﴾ اِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًاۗ ﴿٦﴾
Fa inna ma'al-'usri yusraa (5) Inna ma'al-'usri yusraa (6)
"Maka sesungguhnya beserta kesulitan ada kemudahan, (5) sesungguhnya beserta kesulitan itu ada kemudahan. (6)" (QS Asy-Syarh [94]: 5–6)
Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Demikianlah nikmat-nikmat Allah kepada Nabi. Maka tetaplah optimis dan berharap pada pertolongan Tuhan, karena sesungguhnya beserta kesulitan apa pun pasti ada kemudahan. Penegasan ini diulang: sesungguhnya beserta kesulitan itu pasti ada kemudahan. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 5–6)
3. Perintah Ibadah & Orientasi kepada Allah (7–8)
فَاِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانْصَبْۙ ﴿٧﴾ وَاِلٰى رَبِّكَ فَارْغَبْ ࣖ ﴿٨﴾
Fa idzaa faraghta fanshab (7) Wa ilaa rabbika farghab (8)
"Maka apabila engkau telah selesai (dari sesuatu urusan), tetaplah bekerja keras (untuk urusan yang lain), (7) dan hanya kepada Tuhanmulah engkau berharap. (8)" (QS Asy-Syarh [94]: 7–8)
Tafsir Ringkas Kemenag: Apabila engkau telah selesai dari suatu urusan, tetaplah bekerja keras untuk urusan yang lain; bila selesai dengan urusan dunia atau dakwah, bergegaslah beribadah. Dan hanya kepada Tuhanmulah engkau berharap dengan selalu bertawakal serta mengharap rahmat dan rida-Nya. 📖 Baca tafsir Kemenag (Ayat 7–8)

Pelajaran Spiritual dari Surah Asy-Syarh

  • Akidah: Keyakinan bahwa Allah Maha Meringankan, setiap kesulitan pasti ada kemudahan.
  • Ibadah: Salat dan ibadah sebagai bentuk syukur setelah selesai dari urusan dunia.
  • Akhlak: Keseimbangan antara kerja keras dan tawakal, serta optimisme dalam menghadapi ujian.

Hubungan dengan Surah Lain

  • Dengan Surah Ad-Dhuha (93): Ad-Dhuha memberikan penghiburan dan janji kepuasan, Al-Insyirah menjelaskan mekanisme pelapangan dada dan prinsip kesulitan-kemudahan.
  • Dengan Surah Al-Baqarah (2:286): Keduanya menegaskan Allah tidak membebani seseorang melampaui kemampuannya.
  • Dengan Surah Ar-Ra'd (13:28): Keduanya menekankan ketenangan hati dengan mengingat Allah.
  • Dengan Surah At-Tin (95): Al-Insyirah berbicara tentang pelapangan dada individu, At-Tin tentang keutamaan manusia secara umum.
Sebelumnya: Adh-Dhuha (93) Selanjutnya: At-Tin (95)

Surah Asy-Syarh (94) - Thematic Tafsir

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ
Bismillāhir-raḥmānir-raḥīm
"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."
اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَۙ ﴿١﴾ وَوَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَۙ ﴿٢﴾ الَّذِيْٓ اَنْقَضَ ظَهْرَكَۙ ﴿٣﴾ وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَۗ ﴿٤﴾
Alam nashraḥ laka ṣadrak (1) Wa waḍa‘nā ‘anka wizrak (2) Al-ladhī anqaḍa ẓahrak (3) Wa rafa‘nā laka dhikrak (4)
"Did We not expand for you, [O Muhammad], your breast? (1) And We removed from you your burden (2) Which had weighed upon your back (3) And raised high for you your repute. (4)" (Asy-Syarh 94:1–4)

Overview of Surah Asy-Syarh (94)

Surah Al-Insyirah, also known as Ash-Sharh, is the 94th chapter of the Qur'an consisting of 8 verses. It is categorized as Makki (revealed in Mecca) and is a very meaningful short surah. The name "Al-Insyirah" means "The Expansion" or "The Relief" which refers to the opening of Prophet Muhammad's breast. It thematically covers the expansion of the Prophet's breast, the removal of heavy burden, the principle of difficulty and ease being paired, and encouragement to worship after difficulty passes. This surah was revealed as a continuation of the consolation in Surah Ad-Dhuha, giving assurance that every difficulty is certainly followed by ease.

Revelation Context

Revealed in Mecca after Ad-Duha, when the Prophet faced heavy pressure in his mission. The two surahs complement each other in consoling and strengthening him.

Location: Mecca (Makki)
Period: Early Meccan period
Background: The weight of the mission
Purpose: Strengthen the Prophet and instil optimism
Aqidah (Creed): Belief that Allah lightens burdens; every hardship brings ease.
Ibadah (Worship): Prayer and worship as gratitude after completing worldly tasks.
Akhlaq (Character): Balance between hard work and reliance on Allah; optimism in trials.

Main Themes and Detailed Tafsir

1. Chest Expansion & Burden Removal (1–4)
اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَۙ ﴿١﴾ وَوَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَۙ ﴿٢﴾ الَّذِيْٓ اَنْقَضَ ظَهْرَكَۙ ﴿٣﴾ وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَۗ ﴿٤﴾
Alam nashraḥ laka ṣadrak (1) Wa waḍa‘nā ‘anka wizrak (2) Al-ladhī anqaḍa ẓahrak (3) Wa rafa‘nā laka dhikrak (4)
"Did We not expand for you, [O Muhammad], your breast? (1) And We removed from you your burden (2) Which had weighed upon your back (3) And raised high for you your repute. (4)" (Asy-Syarh 94:1–4)
Tafsir In-depth Tafsir by IslamicStudies.info:

Allah bestows three great blessings upon His Prophet in these verses. First, "sharhus-sadr" (expansion of the breast) is a metaphor for the acceptance of truth, tranquility of heart, breadth of knowledge, and the ability to receive revelation and face the difficulties of dawah with patience. This expansion was a special gift that enabled the Prophet to bear the immensely heavy burden of prophethood.

Second, Allah lifted the "wizr" (burden) that weighed down the Prophet's back. This burden includes the sins before prophethood (though prophets are protected from major sins, this refers to the psychological burden of the pre-Islamic era), or more precisely, the heavy burden of the prophetic mission and the pressure from his people. Allah removed this burden so that the Prophet could carry out his mission with ease.

Third, Allah elevated the Prophet's name in this world and the next. In the shahadah, the Prophet's name is mentioned alongside Allah's name. In the adhan, iqamah, sermons, and every prayer, the name of Muhammad is mentioned with utmost honor. No historical figure's name is mentioned and honored every day by billions of people as is the case with Prophet Muhammad. This is the tangible fulfillment of Allah's promise to raise his status and name.

2. Principle of Difficulty and Ease (5–6)
فَاِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًاۙ ﴿٥﴾ اِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًاۗ ﴿٦﴾
Fa inna ma‘al-‘usri yusrā (5) Inna ma‘al-‘usri yusrā (6)
"For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease. (5) Indeed, with hardship [will be] ease. (6)" (Asy-Syarh 94:5–6)
Tafsir In-depth Tafsir by IslamicStudies.info:

These two verses constitute one of Allah's most comforting and fundamental promises in the Qur'an. The repetition of the same verse (with a slight variation in the initial "fa" in the first verse as a conjunction) serves as extremely strong emphasis. This is Allah's unchanging law: every hardship is certainly accompanied by ease.

The key word here is "ma'a" (with). Ease does not come after the hardship ends; rather, it accompanies the hardship itself. This means that even in the midst of difficult times, elements of ease already exist, even if they are not perceived. Psychologically, this promise provides strength and optimism that difficult situations will not last forever and that there is always a way out.

Scholars explain that one hardship will not overcome two eases (the word 'usri appears in singular form, while yusra appears in definite form indicating multiple ease). This is the foundation of mental resilience in Islam. This promise is also glad tidings that behind every trial, Allah has prepared solutions and ways out.

3. Command for Worship & Reliance on Allah (7–8)
فَاِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانْصَبْۙ ﴿٧﴾ وَاِلٰى رَبِّكَ فَارْغَبْ ࣖ ﴿٨﴾
Fa idhā faraghta fanṣab (7) Wa ilā rabbika farghab (8)
"So when you have finished [your duties], then stand up [for worship]. (7) And to your Lord direct [your] longing. (8)" (Asy-Syarh 94:7–8)
Tafsir In-depth Tafsir by IslamicStudies.info:

This verse provides practical guidance on time management and life orientation. After completing a task – whether a worldly matter like dawah, struggle, work, or even after overcoming difficulty – one should immediately move on to another task with earnestness (nashab). This teaches a high work ethic, not being lazy, and staying productive.

The word "faraghta" linguistically means being free from one task, and "fanshab" means to strive or work hard for the next task. It can also be interpreted: after finishing ritual worship (prayer), then strive in supplication or in worldly matters. However, the broader meaning is that life continues in a cycle from one responsibility to another without pause.

Then, Allah commands to direct all hope and desire only to Him. "Farghab" comes from "raghbah" which means inclination of the heart, hope, and strong desire. This is the essence of tawakkul: after maximum effort, leave the results to Allah and do not hope in anyone else. The combination of "fanshab" (work hard) and "farghab" (hope in Allah) is the perfect balance between striving and reliance on Allah.

Spiritual Lessons from Surah Asy-Syarh

  • Aqidah (Creed): Belief that Allah lightens burdens; every hardship brings ease.
  • Ibadah (Worship): Prayer and worship as gratitude after completing worldly tasks.
  • Akhlaq (Character): Balance between hard work and reliance on Allah; optimism in trials.

Relationship with Other Surahs

  • With Ad-Dhuha (93): Ad-Dhuha provides consolation and promise; Al-Insyirah explains the mechanism of chest expansion and the difficulty-ease principle.
  • With Al-Baqarah (2:286): Both affirm that Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity.
  • With Ar-Ra'd (13:28): Both emphasize heart tranquility through remembrance of Allah.
  • With At-Tin (95): Al-Insyirah focuses on individual expansion; At-Tin on human excellence in general.
